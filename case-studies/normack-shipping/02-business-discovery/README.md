# Phase 02 — Business Discovery & Current-State Assessment

**Case study:** [NorMack Shipping LLC](../README.md)  
**Portfolio status:** Planned — working specification; not a completed client engagement  
**Data classification:** Fictional enterprise and synthetic data only

> **Portfolio note:** This page is a planned phase specification. Activities, findings, solutions and outcomes will be marked complete only after supporting artifacts are produced and validated. No real client contracts, operating results or confidential records are represented.

## Purpose

Investigate operational symptoms and collect evidence across the four service lines without assuming that reported symptoms are root causes.

## NorMack scenario context

The CEO reports recurring schedule changes, manual dispatch coordination, limited visibility into in-transit cargo, inconsistent job costing for crates/pallets, and delays reconciling invoices. These are **scenario prompts to investigate**, not verified findings.

## Questions this phase will answer

Which observed defects are process failures, data-quality failures, resource constraints, or external dependencies?

## Work plan

1. Conduct simulated stakeholder interviews with CEO, dispatcher, drivers, warehouse, assembly, billing, and customers
2. Build AS-IS maps for quote-to-cash, order-to-delivery, receive-store-release, procure-to-assemble, and incident handling
3. Define baseline KPIs: on-time pickup/delivery, dwell time, missed scans, first-attempt delivery, material availability, gross margin by service
4. Request and profile synthetic records and audit events; distinguish system timestamps from human-entered timestamps
5. Perform root-cause analysis using 5 Whys/fishbone and cross-functional dependency mapping
6. Assess process maturity, internal controls, capacity bottlenecks, and gaps in role ownership

## Deliverables and evidence checklist

- [ ] Discovery interview guide and evidence register
- [ ] AS-IS BPMN/swimlane process maps
- [ ] Gap and maturity assessment
- [ ] Preliminary KPI catalog and baseline approach
- [ ] Root-cause hypotheses, issues and risk logs
