# Phase 09 — Executive Storytelling & Recommendations

**Case study:** [NorMack Shipping LLC](../README.md)  
**Portfolio status:** Planned — working specification; not a completed client engagement  
**Data classification:** Fictional enterprise and synthetic data only

> **Portfolio note:** This page is a planned phase specification. Activities, findings, solutions and outcomes will be marked complete only after supporting artifacts are produced and validated. No real client contracts, operating results or confidential records are represented.

## Purpose

Present the investigation, validated findings, solution decisions and simulated outcomes as a concise decision narrative.

## NorMack scenario context

The final presentation should connect the initial CEO concerns to operational evidence, cross-functional causes, options considered, solutions delivered and outcome measures. Never present modeled improvements as real company results.

## Questions this phase will answer

Which decision should management make, why, and what evidence supports it?

## Work plan

1. Create an executive problem-to-evidence-to-decision storyline
2. Build a KPI scorecard comparing baseline, pilot/simulation, and targets with correct labels
3. Present a scenario analysis for volume growth, capacity pressure, cost inflation and delivery risk
4. Deliver interactive PowerPoint/browser presentation and an accessible short executive summary
5. Explain cost-benefit tradeoffs, residual risks, rollout phases, governance and next-step decisions
6. Link each claim to case-study evidence, code, datasets or acceptance-test outcomes

## Deliverables and evidence checklist

- [ ] Executive brief and decision memo
- [ ] Interactive PowerPoint or browser presentation
- [ ] Before/after and scenario scorecards
- [ ] Business case, roadmap, recommendations and risk register
- [ ] Final portfolio walkthrough and evidence index

## Completion and review criteria

- All listed deliverables have working links and clear version/status labels.
- Business assertions cite scenario records, analysis or approved assumptions.
- Requirements and technical artifacts use consistent IDs and links to dependent phases.
- Any public dataset uses synthetic records only; no secrets, customer data or company documents.
- Unfinished functionality is labeled **Planned**, **Prototype**, or **Simulation** rather than complete.

**Expected phase outcome:** Credible, executive-ready narrative that links decisions to documented synthetic evidence.
