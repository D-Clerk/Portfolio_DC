# Phase 08 — Business Intelligence, Analytics & Forecasting

**Case study:** [NorMack Shipping LLC](../README.md)  
**Portfolio status:** Planned — working specification; not a completed client engagement  
**Data classification:** Fictional enterprise and synthetic data only

> **Portfolio note:** This page is a planned phase specification. Activities, findings, solutions and outcomes will be marked complete only after supporting artifacts are produced and validated. No real client contracts, operating results or confidential records are represented.

## Purpose

Build trustworthy decision-support products on validated enterprise datasets.

## NorMack scenario context

Reports should span shipment performance, pickup and delivery punctuality, storage dwell and utilization, packaging throughput, revenue, service profitability, cash conversion, and demand forecasting. KPI definitions must be consistent across operations and finance.

## Questions this phase will answer

What can leadership reliably know, what is still uncertain, and which changes merit action?

## Work plan

1. Build Power BI sales and service performance dashboards with interactive slicing and drill-through
2. Create financial reporting for revenue, job cost, gross margin, budget versus actual and cash-related measures
3. Build star-schema semantic models and document DAX measures, filter context and time intelligence
4. Demonstrate Power Query M cleaning, R/Python analytical treatments and forecast comparisons
5. Create scenarios for fuel, labor, capacity, volume, storage charges and service-mix changes
6. Validate every executive metric against SQL/source-level reconciliation results

## Deliverables and evidence checklist

- [ ] Operations and sales dashboards
- [ ] Financial reporting and profitability model
- [ ] Forecasting and what-if scenario models
- [ ] Documented DAX/M/R calculations and semantic model
- [ ] Metric dictionary and reconciliation evidence

## Completion and review criteria

- All listed deliverables have working links and clear version/status labels.
- Business assertions cite scenario records, analysis or approved assumptions.
- Requirements and technical artifacts use consistent IDs and links to dependent phases.
- Any public dataset uses synthetic records only; no secrets, customer data or company documents.
- Unfinished functionality is labeled **Planned**, **Prototype**, or **Simulation** rather than complete.

**Expected phase outcome:** Interactive, correctly validated analytical outputs, with data lineage and caveats.
