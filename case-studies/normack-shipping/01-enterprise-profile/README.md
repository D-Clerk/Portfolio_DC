# Phase 01 — Enterprise Profile

**Case study:** [NorMack Shipping LLC](../README.md)  
**Portfolio status:** Planned — working specification; not a completed client engagement  
**Data classification:** Fictional enterprise and synthetic data only

> **Portfolio note:** This page is a planned phase specification. Activities, findings, solutions and outcomes will be marked complete only after supporting artifacts are produced and validated. No real client contracts, operating results or confidential records are represented.

## Purpose

Establish the operating model for NorMack Shipping LLC before evaluating systems or prescribing improvements.

## NorMack scenario context

NorMack is a **fictional 15-person enterprise**, including the CEO, providing contract pickup and delivery of air and ground cargo, LTL/freight support, temporary in-transit storage, hybrid corrugated/wood-framed crates, and interlocking specialty pallets. The service lines share limited staff, vehicles, staging space, materials, and administrative capacity. DHL-like customers are **illustrative customer types**, not claimed contracts.

## Questions this phase will answer

What services are promised, who performs each handoff, what assets are shared, and where is operational accountability unclear?

## Work plan

1. Define the service catalog: pickup/delivery, in-transit cargo holding, hybrid crate assembly, specialty pallet assembly
2. Establish a proposed organizational chart, role assignments, segregation-of-duties risks, and cross-trained responsibilities
3. Map locations, storage zones, vehicle classes, capacity constraints, and operating hours
4. Identify customer types, contract/service-level categories, suppliers, and external carriers
5. Inventory candidate applications, spreadsheets, manual records, and data handoffs
6. Define a preliminary glossary and identifiers for shipment, stop, customer, crate job, pallet job, and invoice

## Deliverables and evidence checklist

- [ ] Enterprise profile and service catalog
- [ ] Proposed 15-person organization and RACI matrix
- [ ] Facilities, vehicles and equipment register
- [ ] Stakeholder and system inventory
- [ ] Operating assumptions and data glossary
