# Phase 03 — Requirements & Business Case

**Case study:** [NorMack Shipping LLC](../README.md)  
**Portfolio status:** Planned — working specification; not a completed client engagement  
**Data classification:** Fictional enterprise and synthetic data only

> **Portfolio note:** This page is a planned phase specification. Activities, findings, solutions and outcomes will be marked complete only after supporting artifacts are produced and validated. No real client contracts, operating results or confidential records are represented.

## Purpose

Convert validated operational findings into prioritized requirements and an economically credible improvement proposal.

## NorMack scenario context

Potential solution capabilities include unified shipment tracking, dispatch scheduling, scan-based temporary storage custody, crate/pallet job costing, integrated invoicing, and management reporting. Capabilities are candidates until discovery validates demand.

## Questions this phase will answer

Which capability is essential, which solution is affordable, and how will we prove value?

## Work plan

1. Define stakeholders, goals, business rules, scope boundaries, assumptions, and constraints
2. Produce a BRD with traceable requirement IDs and measurable acceptance conditions
3. Write epics, user stories and Given/When/Then criteria for dispatch, drivers, storage, fabrication, and finance
4. Prioritize using business value, risk, cost, complexity, dependencies, and the limits of a 15-person workforce
5. Evaluate process-only, low-code, custom web, and hybrid solution alternatives
6. Build cost-benefit scenarios and expected-value estimates using explicitly simulated costs and assumptions

## Deliverables and evidence checklist

- [ ] Business Requirements Document (BRD)
- [ ] Epics, user stories, use cases, acceptance criteria
- [ ] Requirements traceability baseline
- [ ] Options analysis and investment business case
