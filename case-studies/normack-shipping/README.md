# NorMack Shipping LLC
## Enterprise Operations, Business Intelligence & Digital Transformation

**An Integrated Business Transformation Portfolio Case Study**

[View Project Roadmap](PROJECT_ROADMAP.md)

### About This Case Study

NorMack Shipping LLC is a fictional small business with approximately 15 employees, including the CEO. The company specializes in freight logistics, cargo pickup and delivery, temporary in-transit storage, custom crating, and specialty pallet assembly.

This portfolio case study demonstrates an end-to-end approach to identifying operational challenges, analyzing business requirements, designing integrated solutions, developing applications, engineering reliable data, and communicating actionable business intelligence.

All company information, employee records, customer transactions, financial results, and operational datasets used in this case study are synthetic. Any references to real-world logistics providers are illustrative and do not imply actual contractual relationships.

### 1. Business Overview

NorMack operates four interconnected service areas:

| Business Area | Primary Activities |
|---|---|
| Freight & Cargo Logistics | Domestic and international cargo support, air and ground freight pickup and delivery, LTL transportation, dispatching, and delivery confirmation |
| Temporary In-Transit Storage | Freight receiving, inspection, staging, location tracking, short-term storage, and release authorization |
| Custom Crating & Packaging | Fabrication and assembly of hybrid corrugated and wood-framed crates, including customized protective packaging |
| Specialty Pallet Assembly | Design and assembly of interlocking and specialty pallets for irregularly shaped or difficult-to-handle cargo |

These operations share personnel, facilities, vehicles, equipment, financial resources, and business information.

### 2. Business Challenge

As a small enterprise managing multiple service lines, NorMack faces the challenge of coordinating operational responsibilities, scheduling, inventory, fabrication, transportation, and financial reporting with limited personnel.

The central business concern is how operational inefficiencies in one area may create delays, additional costs, or inaccurate information across other business functions.

Potential areas of investigation include:

- Dispatching, routing, and delivery performance
- Cargo accountability and temporary-storage tracking
- Resource allocation and employee responsibilities
- Packaging material availability and assembly scheduling
- Vendor management and procurement
- Operational costs, billing, and profitability
- Data consistency, reporting accuracy, and system integration

These are initial areas for investigation, not confirmed findings.

### 3. Business Transformation Objective

Evaluate NorMack's operating environment, identify performance gaps and root causes, recommend improvements, and demonstrate integrated technology solutions that support efficient operations and evidence-based decision-making.

The engagement will follow a structured business solution lifecycle.
